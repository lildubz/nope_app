Privacy Policy for nope.

Last updated: August 5, 2026

nope. ("the App") is developed and published by Orbit ("we," "us," or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information when you use it.

Summary

nope. does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. Everything you enter in the App — habits, streaks, notes, and logs — stays on your device.

Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal data. Specifically:

  • No account required. The App does not ask you to sign up, log in, or provide your name, email, or any other identifying information.
  • No data leaves your device. All habit data, streaks, colors, and logs are stored locally on your device using standard on-device storage (SharedPreferences). We do not have servers that receive or store this information.
  • No analytics or tracking. The App does not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.
  • No third-party sharing. Because we don't collect any data, there is nothing to share with third parties.

Permissions

If the App requests any device permissions (such as notifications), they are used solely to provide in-app functionality (like reminders) and are not used to collect or transmit data off your device.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13. Since no data is collected at all, this applies equally to users of any age.

Data Deletion

Because all data is stored locally on your device, you can delete it at any time by clearing the App's data/storage in your device settings or by uninstalling the App.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
